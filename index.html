About me

01 / PROFILE

I’m a Computer Science graduate from Yusuf Maitama Sule University Kano. My work combines digital data tools with the human side of research: training enumerators, coordinating teams, building trust with communities, and checking the quality of evidence gathered in the field.

I have worked on vaccination coverage, agricultural value chains, electric vehicle adoption, and youth-focused research. I’m most interested in projects where strong fieldwork helps people make better decisions.
